Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 15, 2026

Privacy by Design: OwLlama is built as a "local-only" application. This means that your conversations, AI models, and personal data never leave your device unless you explicitly initiate an external action.

1. Data Collection

OwLlama does NOT collect your personal data on any remote servers. All data processing happens locally on your hardware.

  • Conversations: Stored locally in a secure on-device database.
  • AI Models: Downloaded to your device and executed via local inference.
  • Metadata: App settings and preferences are stored locally.

2. Permissions

To provide a full AI experience, the app may request the following permissions:

  • Microphone: Used only for voice-to-text features. Audio is processed locally.
  • Storage: Used to store and manage AI models and export/import chat history.
  • Notification: Used for background download status and reminders.

3. Third-Party Services

Since OwLlama is 100% local-only, it does not communicate with external AI providers (like OpenAI or OpenRouter) by default. Any model downloads are initiated from public repositories (like Hugging Face) over secure HTTPS connections.

4. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including root/jailbreak detection and encrypted local storage, to protect your data from unauthorized access on your device.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at owpor.dev@gmail.com.
